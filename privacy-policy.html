Privacy Policy — Sinopix

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Sinopix ("the app") is a color-matching and outfit-coordination app. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it's used.

Your photos and closet data

Every photo you take or select, every color you sample, and every item you save to your closet is stored only on your device. None of it is uploaded to us or to any server — the app has no backend and makes no network requests for its own features. If you delete the app, the data stored inside the app is deleted along with it. If you've used the app's built-in Export feature to save a copy beforehand, that exported file exists independently on your device and is not affected by deleting the app.

Your color preferences

If you choose to set color preferences (favorite or "never suggest" color families, or a style lean like bold or tonal), that's stored only on your device, the same way your closet data is. Preferences are only ever set by you, directly, in the Preferences screen — the app never infers them from what you save, generate, or wear.

Your age

The first time you use the app, you're asked a neutral age question. On iPhones that support it, this uses Apple's own Declared Age Range feature, which tells the app only which broad age range you're in (under 13, 13–15, 16–17, or 18 and over) — never your exact birthdate. Otherwise, you enter your birth month and year.

Either answer is stored only on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. It's kept even if you delete and reinstall the app — this is intentional, so the protections below can't be bypassed simply by reinstalling. It's used only to configure how Google AdMob serves ads on your device: Google itself receives only the resulting ad-treatment setting (for example, that child-directed treatment applies), never your actual age or birthdate.

  • Under 13: child-directed ads, and no ad-tracking permission prompt.
  • Under 16 in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland: non-personalized ads.

Sinopix asks its own age question only once. If Apple's feature reported you as under 18, the app checks with Apple again about once a year so these settings can update as you get older — Apple may show its own sharing prompt when it does, or may answer without one, depending on your own device settings. If you decline, your earlier answer is kept. If you entered a birth month and year instead, nothing is asked again — your age is worked out from it.

Your agreement to these documents

The first time you use the app — and again after any major change to the Terms of Service or this policy — you're asked to confirm that you agree to the Terms of Service and have read this Privacy Policy. The app saves which version of each document you accepted, and the date, only on your device. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Unlike your age answer, this record is deleted along with the app, so after a reinstall you'll be asked again.

Advertising

Each time you want the app to generate a new outfit suggestion, you can choose to watch a short rewarded video ad via Google AdMob to unlock that specific generation (assuming you've added enough clothing items to your closet). Watching an ad unlocks one outfit generation at that moment — it doesn't unlock the feature permanently, so you'll see this option again the next time you generate a new outfit. To show these ads, AdMob may collect and use device identifiers and other data as described in Google's Partner Sites policy.

On iOS, you'll be asked for permission before any tracking-based ad personalization occurs, in line with Apple's App Tracking Transparency requirements. You can decline this — the app and its ads will still work, just without personalized targeting.

Camera and photo library access

The app requests camera and photo library access solely so you can photograph or select images of your clothing items. These photos are processed entirely on your device (to sample colors and generate thumbnails) and are never transmitted anywhere by the app itself.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in — there's no personal identity tied to your use of the app.
  • No analytics SDKs beyond what's disclosed above for advertising.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties, beyond the ad SDK's own operation as described above.

Children's privacy

Sinopix isn't aimed specifically at children, but we recognize younger users may use it. That's why the app asks the age question described above before anything else — specifically so we can identify users under 13 and automatically give them a more protective experience: non-personalized, non-tracked ads, and no ad-tracking permission prompt at all. This determination happens, and stays, only on your device — we never receive or see it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above will reflect it. If we make a major change, the next version of the app will ask you to review the updated policy before you continue.
