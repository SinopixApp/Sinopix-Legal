Sinopix — Support

Sinopix is a color-matching and outfit-coordination app for iPhone. If something isn't working, or you have a question, write to us:

SinopixApp@gmail.com

It helps to mention your iPhone model, your iOS version (Settings → General → About), and what you were doing when the problem happened. The app never sends us your data, so we can't see anything on your device — please don't send photos of your closet unless we ask.

Common questions

Where is my closet stored?
Only on your iPhone. There are no accounts and no cloud copy, and your closet is excluded from iCloud backups. Use the Export button regularly if you want a backup, especially before changing phones.

Why am I shown an ad?
To generate an outfit, you can choose to watch a short rewarded video ad shown through Google AdMob.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
Depending on where you live, a "Manage ad privacy choices" button appears in the Closet tab. You can also review the tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
